This Privacy Policy explains how the Divyom mobile application (the “App”), developed and published by Raman Dhiman (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use it. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here.

In short

  • Divyom does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or files.
  • Your bookmarks, jaap counts and app settings are stored only on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device information, such as the Advertising ID, to show and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

a) Information you give us

None. The App has no sign-up, login or forms. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

b) Information stored on your device

To make the App work, some data is saved locally on your phone, such as bookmarked Katha pages, mantra jaap counts and targets, and preferences like sound settings. This data never leaves your device through us, and it is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

c) Information collected automatically by third parties

The App uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the information below. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Data typeCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID and other device identifiersGoogle AdMobShowing ads, frequency capping, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Google AdMobDelivering compatible ads, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itGoogle AdMobShowing locally relevant ads, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (views, taps)Google AdMobAd measurement and reporting
Diagnostics (e.g. ad loading errors, crash data within the ads SDK)Google AdMobKeeping ad delivery working correctly

The App does not collect precise (GPS) location, contacts, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, photos, files, health or financial information.

2. Advertising

Ads in Divyom are served by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use cookies-like identifiers (the Android Advertising ID) to show ads based on your use of this and other apps. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the App will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice at any time.

Learn more about how Google uses information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and user privacy

3. Permissions the App uses

  • Internet and network state — to load ads and content updates.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration (if enabled in the jaap counter) — for tap feedback. This involves no data collection.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, storage, camera or microphone.

4. Children's privacy

Divyom's devotional content is suitable for all ages, but the App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Personalised ads: on your Android phone go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Local data: clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App, to delete all bookmarks, counts and settings.
  • Your requests: you may contact us to ask what information we hold about you or to request deletion. Since we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing to delete on our side; requests about ad data can also be made directly to Google.

6. Data retention and security

We do not run servers that store your personal data. Local data stays on your device until you remove it. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies. Data sent between the App and Google's ad services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, for example if we add new features or services. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Please review it from time to time.
